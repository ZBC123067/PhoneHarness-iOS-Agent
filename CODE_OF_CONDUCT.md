# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in PhoneHarness to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in reviews, issues, and discussions.
- Assume good faith; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Give credit where it is due.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, device evidence, or third-party personal data.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions), and to
public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may warn or block contributors for inappropriate behaviour.
Report concerns privately to the maintainer.
