    Why ZTako Exists

    AI is moving forward.
    Your iPhone should have a path forward too.

    AI phones are evolving from chat toward screen understanding, task collaboration and intelligent execution. But not every iPhone user can — or wants to — follow the newest system version.

    The industry direction

    • Apple Intelligence / Siri AI
    • Samsung Galaxy AI
    • Doubao Phone Assistant
    • nubia AI
    ⚡The gap

    Users who stay behind — on purpose

    • Older iOS versions
    • RootHide · TrollStore
    • Jailbreak tweaks
    • Deep customization

    ZTako explores the third option: for users who keep their environment, an open and customizable personal AI agent — without abandoning the iPhone they chose to keep.

    The future of mobile AI shouldn't be limited to the newest iPhone.
