The AI Experience Gap

New AI phones are arriving.
What about the iPhones left behind?

Across the industry, phones are turning into AI assistants: understanding natural language, reading the screen, and completing tasks. But none of that automatically reaches the iPhones that stay on older iOS.

ZTako explores a new path.

Rather than forcing users to abandon the environment they chose, ZTako explores how a modern AI agent can work with the iPhone they already control — combining jailbreak-grade device access with modern AI models.

One Instruction. Real Action.

Stop telling your phone where to tap.
Tell it what you want to achieve.

ZTako is not just a Q&A chatbot. The goal: you describe the outcome, and the agent observes the screen, understands the UI, plans the steps, asks for authorization where needed, acts, then checks what actually happened.

1Natural languageYou state the goal
2Understand taskParse intent & constraints
3Observe screenRead the live UI
4Identify elementsApps & controls, not pixels
5Plan stepsBreak the goal into actions
6AuthorizeYou approve sensitive actions
7ExecuteTap, swipe, type, launch
8Re-observeLook at the screen again
9Verify resultConfirm the real outcome

Interactive Concept Click any step. The device-control primitives behind this loop (tap, swipe, input, screenshots, OCR, accessibility tree reading) exist today as ZTako's open MCP foundation; the full one-sentence autonomous chain is a development direction.

Local AI + Cloud AI

Your intelligence.
Your choice.

ZTako is designed to separate model capability from execution authority, and to support different model providers over time — local or cloud. No single vendor lock-in, and no automatic upload of your private content.

🖥 Local-first direction

Run understanding on or near your own device where possible. Your screen content and personal data stay with you by default.

  • Privacy by default
  • Works without sending content to third parties
  • Performance depends on local hardware
In Development

☁️ Cloud models

Use stronger cloud models when a task needs them — chosen by you, with clear boundaries on what leaves the device.

  • Highest quality for complex reasoning
  • Your choice of provider, not a mandate
  • Explicit boundaries for private content
In Development

Quality First. Local Preferred. Not all planned model integrations have been validated on the iOS 17 reference device yet; model capability and execution permission remain separate systems.
